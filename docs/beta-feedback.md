# Astrologer beta: what each tester found

One place for every answer from the 10-20 astrologers asked to find a wrong fact (decided by Dafa
29 Oct 2026; `docs/promo-stage-1.md`). Each tester has full access for the beta, given with
`node tools-access-key.mjs issue life "Name"` (the app says "for the beta"). Those who complete
the task become founding testers (CLAUDE.md, Money): mark it in the table.

For every report: the date, the tester, what they compared against (their program, the chart
data), what differed, and what was done about it. A difference is a finding until it is shown to be
the other program's error or a setting, and then it says so.

**The price questions**, asked once at the end of a week of use, in this order, without naming our
price first (decided 29 Oct; why: `docs/pricing-2026-10.md`):

1. At what price would Astrovarium seem so cheap that you would doubt its quality?
2. At what price would it be a bargain, a great buy?
3. At what price would it start to feel expensive, though you might still buy it?
4. At what price would it be too expensive to consider?
5. Would you buy it once at $14.99? At $19.99? At $29.99? At $39.99? (yes or no at each)

Read on 15 Nov 2026 and recorded as **stated intent**, a weak signal: a pre-order or a real payment
outranks it. Three or more "yes" at $29.99 and the paid version goes ahead at that price; a median
"expensive" below $29.99 moves it to $19.99 (CLAUDE.md, Money, point 6).
